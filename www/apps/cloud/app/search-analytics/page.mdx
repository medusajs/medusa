# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn about the Search Analytics section available for your environment's search integration.

## Overview

The Search Analytics section gives you visibility into how search is being used in your environment. It is accessible from an environment's storage and search area in the Cloud dashboard.

The section includes:

- A **Search Volume** chart showing search activity over time.
- A **Top Search Terms** panel listing the most frequently queried text terms.
- A **No-Hit Terms** panel listing text terms that returned no results.
- A **Vector Terms** panel listing the top terms associated with vector-based searches.

---

## Search Volume Chart

The Search Volume chart displays the number of searches performed over a selected time period. The chart shows two separate series:

- **Freetext searches** (blue): text-based queries entered by users.
- **Vector searches** (green): semantic or embedding-based queries.

Both series are stacked on the same chart, so you can compare the volume of each search type and understand the overall search activity in your environment.

---

## Top Search Terms

The **Top Search Terms** panel lists the text terms that appear most frequently in freetext searches. Use this panel to understand what your users are searching for most often.

If there are no freetext terms to display — for example, when all searches in the selected period were vector-based — the panel shows an empty-state message.

---

## No-Hit Terms

The **No-Hit Terms** panel lists text terms that were searched but returned no results. Use this panel to identify gaps in your catalog or search configuration.

If there are no no-hit terms to display, the panel shows an empty-state message.

---

## Vector Terms

The **Vector Terms** panel lists the top search terms associated with vector-based searches in your environment. Use this panel to understand which queries are driving vector search activity.

If there are no vector terms to display, the panel shows an empty-state message.
