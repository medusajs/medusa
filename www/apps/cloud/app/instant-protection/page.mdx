# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn about Medusa Instant Protection and the security features it applies to your Medusa projects on Cloud.

## What is Medusa Instant Protection?

Medusa Instant Protection is a set of security features that Cloud applies to the traffic reaching your Medusa projects. It filters malicious requests, absorbs attacks, and blocks automated abuse before they reach your Medusa application.

When you self-host Medusa, you're responsible for setting up a firewall, mitigating attacks, and keeping up with newly discovered vulnerabilities. On Cloud, Medusa handles this for you, so your projects are protected from the moment they go live without configuring firewall rules, DDoS mitigation, or bot protection yourself.

You can [choose which environments each feature applies to](#manage-features), such as only your Production environment or all environments of your project.

Medusa Instant Protection includes the following features:

- [Medusa Ruleset](#medusa-ruleset): Blocks requests that try to exploit vulnerabilities and attack patterns specific to Medusa applications.
- [Basic Protection Ruleset](#basic-protection-ruleset): Blocks requests that try to exploit common web attacks and widely exploited vulnerabilities.
- [DDoS Protection](#ddos-protection): Detects and mitigates distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks.
- [Bot Detection](#bot-detection): Blocks requests coming from malicious bots.

## Manage Features

Each Medusa Instant Protection feature applies to the environments you select for it. For example, you can apply bot detection to your Production environment only, so that it doesn't interfere with automated tests that send requests to your [preview environments](../environments/preview/page.mdx).

For each feature, you can select:

- **Environment types**: Production, Long-lived, or Previews. The feature applies to every environment of the selected types, including environments you create later.
- **Specific environments**: Individual environments of your project. The feature applies to those environments only.

You can combine both. For example, you can apply a feature to the Production environment type and to a specific [long-lived environment](../environments/long-lived/page.mdx), such as Staging.

To manage the environments of a feature:

1. Go to your project's dashboard.
2. Click **Security**.
3. Click the **Environments** dropdown next to the feature.
4. Select the environments or environment types where you want to enable the feature.

Medusa saves your changes as soon as you select or deselect an option.

## Medusa Ruleset

The Medusa Ruleset is a set of rules that Medusa maintains to protect against vulnerabilities and attack patterns relevant to Medusa applications.

While the [Basic Protection Ruleset](#basic-protection-ruleset) covers general web attacks, the Medusa Ruleset targets threats specific to Medusa. Since Medusa builds both the commerce framework and the Cloud platform, it turns vulnerabilities identified in Medusa into protections enforced at the edge. When Medusa discovers a new attack vector affecting Medusa applications, it deploys a mitigation before malicious traffic reaches your projects.

This protects your projects even before you [update your Medusa application](../update-medusa/page.mdx) to a version that fixes the vulnerability. You should still update your application regularly to receive the fixes in your codebase.

---

## Basic Protection Ruleset

The Basic Protection Ruleset is a managed web application firewall (WAF) ruleset that protects your projects against common web attacks and widely exploited vulnerabilities. It inspects incoming requests, such as their URLs and headers, and blocks requests that match known attack patterns. This stops automated scanners that probe every server on the internet for these vulnerabilities before they reach your Medusa application.

The ruleset receives updates whenever a new wide-ranging vulnerability emerges, so your projects stay protected against new threats without any action on your part. Its rules also aim to minimize false positives, so they don't block legitimate traffic to your Medusa application.

## DDoS Protection

A distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack floods your application with traffic from many sources to make it unavailable to your customers.

Medusa Instant Protection protects your projects against DDoS attacks at both the network and application levels:

- **Network-level attacks**: Floods of traffic that target the network and transport layers, such as TCP-based attacks.
- **Application-level attacks**: Floods of HTTP requests that target your Medusa application or storefront.

DDoS protection detects attacks automatically and mitigates them as they happen, without manual intervention. Legitimate traffic continues to reach your application while Medusa mitigates the attack.

---

## Bot Detection

Bots generate a large share of internet traffic. While some bots are useful, such as search engine crawlers, others scrape your data, try to take over customer accounts, or overload your application.

Medusa Instant Protection analyzes each incoming request to determine whether it comes from a human or an automated source. It blocks requests only when it's certain that they come from a malicious bot, which keeps the risk of blocking real customers low.

Bot detection doesn't block verified bots, such as search engine crawlers, so your storefront's search engine visibility isn't affected.

<Note>

If you have scripts or services that send requests to your Medusa application, make sure they send a `User-Agent` header. Bot detection treats requests with a missing or empty `User-Agent` header as automated and potentially malicious.

</Note>
