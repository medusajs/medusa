# {metadata.title}

Medusa is an AI-native open-source commerce platform for teams and their AI agents to build, launch, and operate reliable commerce applications without worrying about infrastructure.

With Medusa, you get:

- [**Commerce features out of the box**](./features/page.mdx#commerce-features): Products, pricing, carts, orders, payments, inventory, and more, managed by merchants from the Medusa Admin.
- [**Production-grade infrastructure**](./features/page.mdx#infrastructure-features): Medusa deploys your backend, admin, and storefront from your GitHub repository with zero configuration. It runs your database and other resources, creates a preview environment per pull request, deploys without downtime, and autoscales for traffic spikes.
- [**AI agents as first-class users**](./ai-toolkit/page.mdx): Your agents can create your store, build its features, deploy it, and answer questions about its orders and customers.
- [**A flexible Framework**](./features/page.mdx#framework-features): Build the features that make your business unique, such as custom data models, workflows, and integrations, without hard-to-maintain workarounds.

You focus on building your store, on your own or with an AI agent, and Medusa runs it. In this guide, you'll learn how to get started with Medusa.

## Get Started with AI Agents

To get started with an AI agent, run the following prompt in your agent:

```bash title="Prompt"
Fetch https://docs.medusajs.com/start and create an ecommerce store with Medusa Cloud
```

The agent creates your store and helps you build custom features. Learn about the tools it can use in the [AI Toolkit](./ai-toolkit/page.mdx) guide.

---

## Get Started with Medusa

1. [Sign up and create your first project](./first-project/page.mdx) from a starter or your own GitHub repository. New customers in supported regions get a free trial.
2. Customize your store by [setting up your project locally](./setup-locally/page.mdx) or with the [AI Toolkit](./ai-toolkit/page.mdx).
3. Follow the [Going Live Checklist](./going-live/page.mdx) to prepare your store for customers.

---

## Who Should Use Medusa?

Medusa is for businesses of all sizes with unique requirements, from startups building with AI agents to enterprises building complex commerce features. For example:

- A founder who builds with AI agents creates a store on Cloud, connects their agent, and starts selling without managing infrastructure.
- A development team clones their Cloud project, builds custom features with the Framework, and ships each change through preview environments.
- A merchant operates their store from the Medusa Admin and asks AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT about orders and customers.
