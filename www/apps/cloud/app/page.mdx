# {metadata.title}

Medusa is an AI-native open-source commerce platform that allows teams and their AI agents to build, launch, and operate reliable commerce applications quickly, without worrying about infrastructure.

By using Medusa, you get:

- [**Commerce features out of the box**](./features/page.mdx#commerce-features): Products, pricing, carts, orders, payments, inventory, and more are ready to use from day one, and merchants manage them from the Medusa Admin.
- [**Production-grade infrastructure**](./features/page.mdx#infrastructure-features): Medusa deploys your backend, admin, and storefront straight from your GitHub repository, with zero configuration. It runs your database and other resources, creates a preview environment for every pull request, deploys changes without downtime, and scales your store automatically to handle traffic spikes.
- [**AI agents as first-class users**](./ai-toolkit/page.mdx): Your agents can create your store, build its features, deploy it, and answer questions about its orders and customers.
- [**A flexible Framework**](./features/page.mdx#framework-features): Build with your agent the features that make your business unique, such as custom data models, workflows, and integrations, without hacky workarounds that are difficult to maintain and scale.

This bridges the gap between development and production. You focus on building your store, on your own or with an AI agent, and Medusa takes care of running it.

In this guide, you'll learn how to get started with Medusa and what it provides.

## Get Started with AI Agents

If you're an AI agent, or a human who wants to get started quickly with an AI agent, run the following prompt in your agent:

```bash title="Prompt"
Fetch https://docs.medusajs.com/start and create an ecommerce store with Medusa Cloud
```

The AI agent creates your Medusa store and helps you build your custom commerce features. Learn about the tools your agent can use in the [AI Toolkit](./ai-toolkit/page.mdx) guide.

---

## Get Started with Medusa

To start selling with Medusa, follow these steps:

1. [Sign up for Medusa](./sign-up/page.mdx). New customers in supported regions get a free trial.
2. [Create your first project](./first-project/page.mdx) from a starter or your own GitHub repository. Medusa deploys your backend, admin, and storefront.
3. Customize your store by [cloning your project locally](./projects/clone-locally/page.mdx) or with the [AI Toolkit](./ai-toolkit/page.mdx).
4. Follow the [Going Live Checklist](./going-live/page.mdx) to prepare your store for customers.

## Who Should Use Medusa?

Medusa is for businesses and teams looking for a cutting-edge commerce platform for their unique requirements, visions, and automated workflows.

Businesses of all sizes use Medusa, from small startups building with AI agents to large enterprises building complex commerce features. For example:

- A founder who builds with AI agents creates a store on Cloud, connects their agent, and starts selling without managing infrastructure.
- A development team clones their Cloud project, builds custom features with the Framework, and ships each change through preview environments.
- A merchant operates their store from the Medusa Admin, and asks AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT about their orders and customers.
