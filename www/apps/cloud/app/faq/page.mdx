# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll find answers to frequently asked questions about getting started with Medusa.

## Can I deploy a Medusa store for free?

Medusa provides a free trial for select regions. If you're eligible for a free trial, you can start it when you [sign up for Medusa](../sign-up/page.mdx). During the trial, you can deploy your Medusa store, including its backend, admin, and storefront, at no cost. You enter your payment details when you sign up, but Medusa doesn't charge you until the trial ends.

While your organization is on a free trial, the Cloud dashboard shows a banner with the time left in your trial and the date billing starts. Learn how to view your trial's status in the [Manage Plans](../billing/plans/page.mdx#view-trial-status) guide.

Medusa is also open source, so you can use it for free on your own infrastructure. Learn more in the [next question](#is-medusa-open-source).

---

## Is Medusa open source?

Yes, Medusa's commerce features and Framework are open source and available under the MIT license in the [Medusa GitHub repository](https://github.com/medusajs/medusa). You can use them for free, and you own your project's code and data.

You pay for Medusa when you deploy your store with its managed infrastructure, which gives you hosting, managed resources, and services like emails and search without setting them up yourself. To learn how that compares to hosting Medusa yourself, refer to the [Cloud vs Self-Hosted](../comparison/page.mdx) guide.

Some features, such as role-based access control (RBAC) and single sign-on (SSO), aren't part of the open source license. They're available with the Enterprise plan or an Enterprise license.

---

## What happens when my free trial ends?

When your trial ends, billing starts automatically. Medusa charges the payment method you added during sign-up for your plan, and your projects keep running without interruption.

If you don't want to continue after the trial, you can [cancel your organization's plan](../billing/plans/page.mdx#cancelling-your-organizations-plan).

If your organization doesn't have a payment method, for example, because you removed it, the trial banner shows an **Add your billing details to keep your projects** link. Click it to [add a payment method](../billing/manage/page.mdx#add-payment-method-to-organization) before the trial ends.

If your trial already ended without a payment method, you can still keep your plan from the billing settings. Learn more in the [Resume your Organization's Plan](../billing/plans/page.mdx#resume-your-organizations-plan) guide.

## Do I need to be a developer to use Medusa?

You can create and deploy a store from a starter without writing code. To add customizations for your business needs, you need a developer who is familiar with Medusa, or an AI agent with the [AI Toolkit](../ai-toolkit/page.mdx). Learn about Medusa in the [Getting Started](!docs!/learn) guide.

You can also contact a [Medusa Expert](https://medusajs.com/experts/) to help you with building and deploying your Medusa project.

---

## Does Medusa host storefronts?

Yes, Medusa hosts storefronts alongside your Medusa backend and admin dashboard in a monorepo structure. Medusa supports storefronts built with Next.js, SvelteKit, and Tanstack Start, with automatic configuration and deployment for each environment.

Refer to the [Create Project](../projects/page.mdx) to learn more.

---

## What resources does Medusa manage for my project?

Medusa fully-manages your [PostgreSQL databases](../database/page.mdx), [Redis instances](../redis/page.mdx), and [S3 buckets](../s3/page.mdx). You don't need to configure or maintain them.

---

## Do I own my Medusa project's code and data?

Yes, your code is hosted on your GitHub repository. You can also export a dump of your project's database at any time.

---

## Can I deploy multiple Medusa projects?

Yes, if your plan supports multiple projects. Learn more in the [Plans & Pricing](../pricing/page.mdx) guide.

---

## Can I use my custom plugins and modules?

Yes, you can use any custom and out-of-the-box plugins and modules in your deployed Medusa project. You retain the same flexibility as you would with a self-hosted Medusa project.

You can deploy projects like marketplaces, B2B, subscription-based stores, and more.

---

## Does Medusa create a new project for each deployment?

No, Medusa does not create new Medusa projects for your deployments. Instead, it deploys your existing Medusa project from your GitHub repository.

---

## Can I use a static IP address for my Medusa project?

Medusa uses static IP addresses for outgoing requests from your Medusa backend to external services, and dynamic IP addresses for incoming requests to your Medusa backend. You can use the static outgoing IP addresses to whitelist them in your firewall or allowlist them in external services.

You can find the list of static outgoing IP addresses in the [IP Addresses](../ip-addresses/page.mdx) guide.

---

## Can I password-protect my deployment or preview URLs?

No, Medusa doesn't have a built-in option to password-protect or otherwise restrict access to the URLs of long-lived or preview environments. The Medusa Admin and admin API routes already require authentication, but the storefront is publicly accessible.

To keep a storefront private while you test it, you can add HTTP basic authentication in your storefront's code. Learn how in the [Restrict Access to a Deployment](../deployments/access/page.mdx#restrict-access-to-a-deployment) guide.

---

## How do I know if Medusa has an ongoing issue?

Check the [Cloud status page](https://status.medusajs.com) for the current health of Cloud's services and a history of past incidents. You can also subscribe to incident updates by email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or RSS.

Learn more in the [Cloud Status Page](../status/page.mdx) guide.

---

## My card from India is not being accepted. What should I do?

Cards issued by banks in India are not supported. Please use cards issued by banks in other countries.

You can also [contact support](https://medusajs.com/contact/) to discuss alternative payment methods for Pro and Enterprise plans.
