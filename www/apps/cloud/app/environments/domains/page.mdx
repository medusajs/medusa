In this guide, you'll learn how to configure custom domains for your environments in Cloud.

## Custom Domains Overview

Cloud allows you to configure custom domains for both your Medusa backend and storefront. This lets you use your own domain names instead of the default Cloud-provided URLs.

There are two types of domain configurations available:

- **Medusa Subdomains**: Configure subdomains for your backend and storefront using Medusa's domain (e.g., `mystore.medusajs.app`)
- **Custom Domains**: Use your own domain names for both backend and storefront (e.g., `api.mystore.com`, `mystore.com`)

<Note type="warning">

Custom domains are not available for **Free** and **Developer** plans. You need to upgrade to **Launch**, **Scale**, or **Enterprise** plans to use this feature.

</Note>

---

## Configure Medusa Subdomains

Medusa-hosted subdomains allow you to use custom subdomain names while keeping them under the Medusa domain.

To configure Medusa subdomains:

1. Open your project in the Cloud dashboard.
2. Click on the environment you want to configure.
3. In the environment settings, navigate to the **Domains** section.
4. In the **Medusa domains** section:
   - Enter your desired **Backend subdomain** (e.g., `mystore-api`)
   - Enter your desired **Storefront subdomain** (e.g., `mystore`)
5. Click **Save** to apply the changes.

Your backend will be accessible at `{backend-subdomain}.medusajs.app` and your storefront at `{storefront-subdomain}.medusajs.app`.

## Configure Custom Domains

Custom domains allow you to use your own domain names for your Medusa backend and storefront.

### Add a Custom Domain

To add a custom domain:

1. Open your project in the Cloud dashboard.
2. Click on the environment you want to configure.
3. In the environment settings, navigate to the **Domains** section.
4. In the **Custom domains** section, click **Add domain**.
5. Enter your custom domain name (e.g., `api.mystore.com` for backend or `mystore.com` for storefront).
6. Select whether this domain is for your **Backend** or **Storefront**.
7. Click **Add** to save the domain.

### DNS Verification

After adding a custom domain, you need to verify that you own the domain by configuring DNS records.

Cloud will provide you with DNS verification instructions that typically include:

- **DNS record type**: Usually a CNAME or A record
- **Host/Name**: The record name to create in your DNS provider
- **Value**: The target value for the DNS record

To complete DNS verification:

1. Log in to your DNS provider's dashboard (where you manage your domain).
2. Create the DNS record as instructed by Cloud.
3. Wait for DNS propagation (this can take up to 24 hours).
4. Return to the Cloud dashboard to check the verification status.

The domain status will show as:

- **Pending**: DNS verification is in progress
- **Configured**: DNS records are set up correctly
- **Verified**: Domain is fully configured and active

### Backend Custom Domains

<Note type="warning">

Backend custom domains are currently behind a feature flag and may not be available to all users yet.

</Note>

Backend custom domains allow you to use your own domain for your Medusa API endpoints. Once configured, your admin dashboard and API will be accessible through your custom domain.

### Remove a Custom Domain

To remove a custom domain:

1. In the **Custom domains** section, find the domain you want to remove.
2. Click the remove or delete button next to the domain.
3. Confirm the removal in the popup dialog.

<Note>

Removing a custom domain will immediately stop routing traffic from that domain to your environment. Make sure to update any applications or services that depend on the domain before removing it.

</Note>

## Domain Management Best Practices

When configuring custom domains, consider these best practices:

- **Use descriptive subdomains**: Choose clear subdomain names that indicate their purpose (e.g., `api.mystore.com` for backend, `shop.mystore.com` for storefront).
- **Plan for multiple environments**: If you have staging and production environments, use different subdomains or domains for each (e.g., `staging-api.mystore.com`, `api.mystore.com`).
- **Monitor DNS propagation**: DNS changes can take time to propagate. Allow up to 24 hours for full propagation.
- **Keep records updated**: If you change hosting providers or DNS settings, make sure to update your custom domain configurations in Cloud.

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues with custom domains:

- **Domain verification fails**: Double-check that your DNS records match exactly what Cloud provided, including any trailing dots or specific formatting.
- **Domain shows as pending**: DNS propagation can take time. Wait a few hours and check again.
- **Site not accessible**: Ensure your DNS records are correctly configured and have had time to propagate.
- **SSL certificate issues**: Cloud automatically provides SSL certificates for verified domains. If you see SSL errors, contact support.
