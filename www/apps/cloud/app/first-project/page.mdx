# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn how to sign up for Medusa, set up your organization, and create your first project from a starter or from your own GitHub repository.

<Note title="Free Trial Availability" type="soon">

The free trial is available only in select regions. You enter payment details, but Medusa doesn't charge you until the trial ends.

</Note>

<Note>

To create a new organization with an existing account, refer to the [Create an Organization](../organizations/page.mdx#create-or-join-an-organization) guide. If you already have an organization, skip to [Step 4](#step-4-create-your-first-project).

</Note>

## Step 1: Create a Medusa Account

Go to [cloud.medusajs.com/signup](https://cloud.medusajs.com/signup?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=cloud_signup_guide) and sign up with your GitHub account or email address.

---

## Step 2: Set Up Your Account

Enter your account details, which Medusa also uses for your organization:

- **What should we call you?**: Enter your full name. Medusa also uses it as the organization's name, which you can change later in the [organization settings](../organizations/page.mdx#edit-organization-details).
- **Where did you hear about Medusa?**: Select an option from the dropdown.
- **What are you building?**: Select an option from the dropdown. If none fits, select "Other" and describe it in the text box that appears.
- **News and features**: Keep the switch enabled to receive updates about new features, improvements, and other news, or disable it to opt out.

Then, click **Next**.

---

## Step 3: Add Billing Details

Add your organization's payment and billing details. The top of the page shows the plan you're signing up for and its price. To choose a different plan, click **Change plan**.

If you're on a free trial, your first payment is due when the trial ends. The **Payment details** section shows that date.

Enter the following details:

- **Card information**: Enter your card's number, expiry date, and CVC.
- **Legal name**: Enter the name to bill, such as your company's legal name. It defaults to your organization's name.
- **Billing email**: Enter the email address that receives invoices and billing notifications. It defaults to your account's email address.
- **Tax ID**: Enter your tax ID for tax purposes, if you have one.
- **Country**, **Address**, **City**, and **Postal code**: Enter your billing address. Medusa uses it for payments and invoices.

<Note title="Restrictions">

Medusa doesn't support cards issued by banks in India. Use a card issued by a bank in another country.

</Note>

Click **Start your free trial** or **Start your $29/mo plan**, based on your plan. Medusa creates your organization and redirects you to its projects list.

You can [view a free trial's status](../billing/plans/page.mdx#view-trial-status) in the organization's billing settings. To keep your plan after the trial ends, refer to [Resume your Organization's Plan](../billing/plans/page.mdx#resume-your-organizations-plan).

---

## Step 4: Create Your First Project

When your organization has no projects, the projects list shows two options to create one. Both result in a deployed Medusa store:

1. [Browse starters](#option-1-choose-a-starter): Start from a Medusa-built starter for DTC, B2B, and more.
2. [Import repository](#option-2-import-a-repository): Import a Medusa project hosted on your GitHub account or organization.

### Option 1: Choose a Starter

<RolesBadge roles={["developer"]} />

A starter gives you an ecommerce store with the necessary foundation, which you then customize for your business. To create a project from one:

1. In the projects list, click the **Browse starters** card.
2. Find the starter that fits your use case.
    - To show only starters of a specific category, click the **DTC** or **B2B** filter button. Click **All** to show all starters again.
    - To view a starter's details, such as its features and storefront framework, click its **Preview** button.
3. Click the starter's **Deploy** button.
4. Follow the project creation steps. Refer to the [Projects guide](../projects/page.mdx#1-create-project-from-a-starter) for details.

Medusa clones the starter into a GitHub repository in your account or organization. Once the project deploys, you can start selling. To customize the store, refer to the [Set Up Medusa Locally](../setup-locally/page.mdx) guide.

### Option 2: Import a Repository

<RolesBadge roles={["developer"]} />

To create a project from a Medusa application hosted on GitHub:

1. In the projects list, click the **Import repository** card.
2. Follow the project creation steps, including choosing the repository to import. Refer to the [Projects guide](../projects/page.mdx#2-create-project-from-an-existing-application) for details.

Once the project deploys, you can start selling.
