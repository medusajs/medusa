# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn how to set up and manage the payment accounts your stores take live payments with on Cloud.

## Payment Accounts Overview

Medusa supports integrated payment accounts for your organization with the following options:

- [Medusa Payments account](./medusa-payments/page.mdx): Medusa creates and runs the Stripe account for you, so you don't need a Stripe account.
- [Stripe account](./stripe/page.mdx): You connect one or more Stripe accounts that you already have, and you manage them in Stripe.
- [Adyen account](./adyen/page.mdx): You register Medusa's Adyen Module Provider in your Medusa application and configure it with your own Adyen account. Cloud doesn't provide an integrated experience for Adyen.

Learn about the differences between them in the [Differences Between Payment Account Types](#differences-between-payment-account-types) section.

<Note title="Required Medusa version">

To take payments with a Medusa Payments account or a connected Stripe account, your Medusa application must use Medusa [v2.23.0](https://github.com/medusajs/medusa/releases/tag/v2.23.0) or later. If your application uses an older version, [update it](!docs!/learn/update) first.

</Note>

## View Payment Accounts

To view your organization's payment accounts:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../../organizations/page.mdx#switch-organization) whose payments you want to manage.
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Payments** > **Overview** in the sidebar.

The **Payment accounts** page shows the payment accounts that you've set up, or the option to set up a new one if your organization has none.

If your organization has more than one payment account, use the selector at the top right of the page to choose the account to view.

<Note>

The **Transactions**, **Payouts**, **Disputes**, **Reports**, and **Payment methods** items in the sidebar are disabled until your organization has a payment account.

</Note>

## View Payment Account Balance

Once a payment account has activity, your organization's **Payments > Overview** page shows the account's balance below its details:

- **Available**: The balance that's available to pay out to your bank account.
- **Pending**: The balance from recent payments that isn't available to pay out yet.
- **Latest payout**: The amount, date, and status of the account's latest payout.

The **Transactions**, **Payouts**, and **Disputes** items in the sidebar show the rest of the account's activity, as explained in the following sections.

---

## View Transactions

You can view the live payments your stores have taken with a payment account across all of your organization's environments.

To view a payment account's transactions:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../../organizations/page.mdx#switch-organization) whose payments you want to manage.
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Payments** > **Transactions** in the sidebar.
4. If your organization has more than one payment account, choose the account to view from the selector at the top right of the page.

The **Transactions** page lists the account's payments with their date, status, customer, and amount. You can:

- Filter the payments by amount, customer email, date, status, or payment method.
- Export the payments.
- Open a payment to view its details, capture it, or refund it.

<Note>

The **Transactions** page doesn't include payments taken in test mode. To view those, check the transactions of the environment that took them. Learn more in the [Set up Medusa Payments in Environments](../environments/page.mdx#view-environment-transactions) guide.

</Note>

To view the transactions of a single environment, use the environment's **Payments** page instead.

---

## View Payouts

You can view the payouts from a payment account to your bank account, and the account's balance. Learn how payouts work in the [How Payouts Work](#how-payouts-work) section.

To view a payment account's payouts:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../../organizations/page.mdx#switch-organization) whose payments you want to manage.
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Payments** > **Payouts** in the sidebar.
4. If your organization has more than one payment account, choose the account to view from the selector at the top right of the page.

The **Payouts** page shows the account's total balance and the amount available to pay out. Click **See details** to view a breakdown of the balance.

Below the balance, the page lists the account's payouts with their date, status, destination bank account, and amount. You can filter the payouts by status, amount, method, or date, and export them.

### How Payouts Work

Stripe pays out a payment account's available balance to the bank account you added for it, on the account's payout schedule:

- **Medusa Payments account**: You add the bank account during [onboarding](./medusa-payments/page.mdx#complete-medusa-payments-onboarding).
- **Your own Stripe account**: The bank account and payout schedule come from your Stripe account, and you manage them in the Stripe dashboard. The **Payouts** page only shows the account's payouts.

A payment account keeps paying out its balance even when it can't take new payments, for example, when your organization moves to the free plan or when you [close a Medusa Payments account](./medusa-payments/page.mdx#close-a-medusa-payments-account).

## View Disputes

A dispute happens when a customer asks their bank to reverse a payment, for example, because they don't recognize it. You can view and respond to disputes on a payment account's payments.

To view a payment account's disputes:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../../organizations/page.mdx#switch-organization) whose payments you want to manage.
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Payments** > **Disputes** in the sidebar.
4. If your organization has more than one payment account, choose the account to view from the selector at the top right of the page.

The **Disputes** page lists the account's disputes with the date to respond by, the dispute date, status, reason, customer, and disputed amount. You can:

- Filter the disputes by reason, status, amount, dispute date, or respond-by date.
- Export the disputes.
- Open a dispute to view its details and respond to it.

Respond to a dispute before its respond-by date. Otherwise, the bank usually decides in the customer's favor.

## Manage Payment Methods

A payment account's payment methods decide how customers can pay at checkout, such as with cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or bank redirects like iDEAL and Klarna. Customers only see the methods that support their cart's currency and their location.

How you manage payment methods depends on the account's type:

- **Medusa Payments account**: You turn payment methods on or off from the Cloud dashboard, as explained below.
- **Your own Stripe account**: You manage payment methods in your Stripe dashboard.

To manage a Medusa Payments account's payment methods:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../../organizations/page.mdx#switch-organization) whose payments you want to manage.
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Payments** > **Payment methods** in the sidebar.
4. Turn the payment methods you want to offer on, and the ones you don't want to offer off.

Your changes apply to the payments your live environments take with the account. Environments in test mode use their test account, which has its own payment methods. Learn how to change them in the [Manage Test Payment Methods](../environments/page.mdx#manage-test-payment-methods) section of the Set up Medusa Payments in Environments guide.

<Note>

Payment methods that complete days after checkout, such as bank debits, bank transfers, and vouchers, aren't available with Medusa Payments yet.

</Note>

### Payment Methods in Your Storefront

Your storefront shows the payment methods you turn on when it takes payments with the Stripe Payment Element, without a fixed list of payment method types. All Medusa starters do this. Learn how to set it up in a custom storefront in the [Add Medusa Payments to Your Storefront](../storefront/page.mdx#step-4-take-payments-with-the-payment-element) guide.

Apple Pay and Google Pay only show on domains registered with the payment account. Medusa registers the domains of the storefronts you deploy on Cloud, which are the storefront's default domain and its verified custom domains, after each deployment and when a custom domain is verified. Storefronts hosted outside Cloud don't show Apple Pay and Google Pay yet.
