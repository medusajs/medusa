## What is Medusa Payments?

Medusa Payments lets your Cloud stores accept payments without installing or configuring a payment provider yourself. Payments are powered by Stripe, and Medusa sets up the payment provider in each environment's Medusa application and storefront.

Medusa Payments includes the following features:

- **Zero configuration**: Cloud configures the payment provider in your Medusa application and sets the publishable key in your deployed storefronts. You don't manage any API keys or webhooks.
- **Test payments in every environment**: Every environment gets its own test account, so you can test your checkout with Stripe's test cards before you set up a payment account and take real payments.
- **Live payments without a Stripe account**: Set up a [Medusa Payments account](./accounts/medusa-payments/page.mdx) that Medusa creates and runs for you, and share your business details with Stripe in a form in the Cloud dashboard.
- **Bring your own Stripe account**: If your business already uses Stripe, [connect your Stripe accounts](./accounts/stripe/page.mdx) and keep managing them in Stripe.
- **Go live with one click**: Choose a payment account on an [environment's **Payments** page](./environments/page.mdx) to take real payments, then redeploy the environment.
- **Payments next to your projects**: View transactions, balances, payouts, and disputes in the Cloud dashboard, alongside your projects and environments.
- **Storefront support in starters**: All Medusa starters support Medusa Payments since [Medusa v2.22.3](https://github.com/medusajs/medusa/releases/tag/v2.22.3). For other storefronts, an [AI agent prompt](./storefront/page.mdx) adds the support for you.

Medusa Payments is available on all plans.

## Get Started with Medusa Payments on Cloud

Every environment has a test account that takes test payments, so you can test your checkout before you set up a payment account. Follow these steps to test payments and take live payments in your store.

### 1. Add Medusa Payments to Your Storefront

Your storefront's checkout must support Medusa Payments, which all starters do since [Medusa v2.22.3](https://github.com/medusajs/medusa/releases/tag/v2.22.3). For older storefronts, follow the [Add Medusa Payments to Your Storefront](./storefront/page.mdx) guide.

### 2. Test Payments in an Environment

Each environment gets its own test account, without you setting up a payment account. The environment is in test mode by default, so it takes payments with its test account, and nothing is charged.

To place a test payment:

1. In the [environment's dashboard](../environments/long-lived/page.mdx#open-environment-dashboard), click **Payments** in the sidebar, and check that the test account under **Test** shows the **Active** badge.
2. [Redeploy the environment](../environments/long-lived/page.mdx#redeploy-environment) so that it takes payments with its test account.
3. In the environment's Medusa Admin, enable the **Medusa Payments** payment provider in the regions where customers can pay with it. Learn how in the [Regions](!user-guide!/settings/regions#edit-region-details) Merchant Guide.
4. Open the environment's storefront, add a product to the cart, and go to checkout.
5. In the payment step, choose **Credit card** and enter one of [Stripe's test cards](https://docs.stripe.com/testing#cards), such as `4242 4242 4242 4242`, with any future expiry date and any CVC.
6. Place the test order.
7. In the [environment's dashboard](../environments/long-lived/page.mdx#open-environment-dashboard), click **Payments** in the sidebar, then scroll down to the **Transactions** section to find the payment.

Learn more in the [Set up Medusa Payments in Environments](./environments/page.mdx#how-test-accounts-work) guide.

### 3. Set Up a Payment Account

To take live payments, your organization needs a payment account. Set up a Medusa Payments account or connect your own Stripe account:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../organizations/page.mdx#switch-organization) whose payments you want to manage.
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Payments** > **Overview** in the sidebar.
4. Choose how to take payments:
   - To have Medusa create and run the Stripe account for you, click **Set up Medusa Payments** in the **Managed** card.
   - To use a Stripe account you already have, click **Connect Stripe** in the **Your Stripe account** card, then choose the account in Stripe.
5. Finish the account's onboarding:
   - For Medusa Payments, click **Add information** in the section below the account's card, and share your business details with Stripe. Learn more in the [Complete Medusa Payments Onboarding](./accounts/medusa-payments/page.mdx#complete-medusa-payments-onboarding) section.
   - For your own Stripe account, click **Open in Stripe** on the account's card if Stripe needs more information. Learn more in the [Finish Setting Up a Connected Stripe Account](./accounts/stripe/page.mdx#finish-setting-up-a-connected-stripe-account) section.
6. Wait until the account shows the **Active** status. Stripe's review can take from a few minutes to a few business days.

Learn more in the [Payment Accounts](./accounts/page.mdx) guide.

### 4. Take Live Payments in an Environment

Only take live payments in environments that customers purchase through, such as your production environment or a long-lived environment. Keep other environments, such as preview environments, on their test account.

To take live payments in an environment:

1. In the [environment's dashboard](../environments/long-lived/page.mdx#open-environment-dashboard), click **Payments** in the sidebar.
2. In the **Account** section, find your payment account under **Live**, and click **Use this account**.
3. Confirm the switch.
4. [Redeploy the environment](../environments/long-lived/page.mdx#redeploy-environment). The environment takes payments with the account from its next deployment.
5. In the environment's Medusa Admin, enable the payment provider in the regions where customers can pay with it. Learn how in the [Regions](!user-guide!/settings/regions#edit-region-details) Merchant Guide. The provider's name is:
   - **Medusa Payments** if you use a Medusa Payments account.
   - **Stripe** if you connected your own Stripe account.

Once the deployment finishes, your store takes real payments from your customers. Learn more in the [Set up Medusa Payments in Environments](./environments/page.mdx#use-a-live-account) guide.

## Payment Accounts

A payment account is the account your stores take live payments with. Medusa supports a [Medusa Payments account](./accounts/medusa-payments/page.mdx) that Medusa creates and runs for you, your own [Stripe accounts](./accounts/stripe/page.mdx), and your own [Adyen account](./accounts/adyen/page.mdx).

Learn more about each option and how to manage them in the [Payment Accounts](./accounts/page.mdx) guide.

---

## Test Mode

Every environment gets its own test account, even before your organization sets up a payment account. While an environment is in test mode, it takes payments with its test account, and no customer is charged. You pay with [Stripe's test cards](https://docs.stripe.com/testing#cards) at checkout to test your payment flows.

When you're ready to take real payments, you switch the environment from its test account to your live payment account.

Learn more in the [Set up Medusa Payments in Environments](./environments/page.mdx#how-test-accounts-work) guide.

---

## Payouts

Stripe pays out the balance of a live payment account to your bank account. You can view the payouts of each payment account in your organization's payment settings.

Learn more in the [How Payouts Work](./accounts/page.mdx#how-payouts-work) section.
