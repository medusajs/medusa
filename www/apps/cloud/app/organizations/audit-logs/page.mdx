In this guide, you'll learn what audit logs are, how to turn them on for your organization, and how to view them.

## What are Audit Logs?

Audit logs record who did what in your organization, on which resource, and how it ended. You can use them to investigate a change, review access, or meet compliance requirements.

When you turn on audit logs, Medusa records two types of activity:

- **Cloud activity**: Actions that members, [organization access keys](../page.mdx), and Medusa support take in your organization through the Cloud dashboard, the [Cloud CLI](../../cli/page.mdx), and the Cloud API. For example, creating an environment or updating an environment variable. You view these logs in the Cloud dashboard.
- **Commerce activity**: Changes made in your environments' Medusa applications, such as creating a product or updating an order from the Medusa Admin. You view these logs in each environment's Medusa Admin.

Each audit log shows:

- **Time**: When the action happened.
- **Actor**: Who performed the action. For members, it's the member's name or email. For access keys, it's the access key's title.
- **Action**: The action that the actor performed.
- **Resource**: The ID or type of the resource that the action affected.
- **Outcome**: Whether the action **Succeeded**, was **Denied**, or **Failed**.

## Audit Logs Pricing and Retention

<Note>

Audit logs are available on the **Scale** and **Enterprise** plans. Learn how to change your plan in the [Manage Plans](../../billing/plans/page.mdx#change-plan) guide.

</Note>

Audit logs are a paid add-on that you turn on per organization. On the Scale plan, audit logs cost $50 per month, plus $0.50 per 1,000 recorded audit logs. You can track the number of recorded audit logs in the [Usage](../../usage/page.mdx) page.

Medusa keeps audit logs for 14 days by default, and older audit logs are no longer visible. The **Audit log** page shows the exact pricing and retention period of your organization's plan before you turn audit logs on.

## Turn On Audit Logs

<RolesBadge roles={["billing"]} />

To turn on audit logs for your organization:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../page.mdx#switch-organization).
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Audit log** in the sidebar.
4. Click the **Turn on audit logs** button.
5. Review the pricing in the confirmation dialog, then click **Turn on**.

Medusa starts recording Cloud activity right away, and starts charging the monthly fee from that day. You only see audit logs recorded after you turned on audit logs.

<Note>

If your organization's plan doesn't include audit logs, the page shows a **Change plan** link instead of the **Turn on audit logs** button.

</Note>

### Record Commerce Activity in Environments

<Note title="Prerequisites">

Environments must run Medusa v2.23.0 or later to record commerce activity.

</Note>

After you turn on audit logs, an environment only starts recording its commerce activity after its next deployment. To start recording commerce activity right away, [redeploy your environments](../../deployments/page.mdx).

## View Cloud Audit Logs

<RolesBadge />

To view your organization's Cloud audit logs:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../page.mdx#switch-organization).
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Audit log** in the sidebar.

The page shows a table of the audit logs, with the most recent first. A **Medusa support** badge next to an actor means that Medusa support took the action while signed in as that actor.

### Filter Audit Logs

You can narrow down the audit logs with the controls above the table:

- **Search**: Search audit logs by action, resource ID, or actor ID.
- **Project**: Show only the audit logs of a project.
- **Outcome**: Show only audit logs with the **Succeeded**, **Denied**, or **Failed** outcome.
- **Time range**: Show audit logs from the last day, 7 days, 30 days, or 90 days, or from a custom range. By default, the page shows audit logs from the last 7 days.

### View an Audit Log's Details

To view an audit log's details, click its row in the table. This expands the row to show the following details:

- **Actor ID**: The ID of the member or access key that performed the action.
- **Resource ID**: The ID of the resource that the action affected.
- **Project**: The project that the action affected, if any. Actions taken on the organization or user level may not have a project.
- **Environment**: The environment that the action affected, if any. Actions taken on the organization or user level may not have an environment.
- **Request**: The API request's method and path.
- **Status code**: The HTTP status code of the API request's response.
- **Duration**: How long the action took, in milliseconds.
- **IP address**: The IP address that the actor sent the request from.
- **User agent**: The browser or tool that the actor used to send the request.
- **Request ID**: The ID of the API request, which is useful when contacting support.

To show all actions of the same actor or on the same resource, click the <InlineIcon Icon={Funnel} alt="filter" /> icon next to the **Actor ID** or **Resource ID** in the expanded row. The applied filter appears above the table, and you can click it to remove it.

## View Commerce Audit Logs

Your environment's Medusa Admin shows the commerce activity of that environment. Medusa records the requests that create, update, or delete data through the Admin API, such as creating a product, updating an order, or signing in.

To view an environment's commerce audit logs:

1. Open the environment's Medusa Admin and log in.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Audit logs**.

Learn more in the [View Audit Logs](!user-guide!/settings/audit-logs) Merchant Guide.

<Note>

Admin users in the Medusa Admin need the `audit_log:read` permission to view audit logs. Learn more about permissions in the [RBAC](!resources!/enterprise/rbac) guide.

</Note>

### Customize Commerce Audit Logs

When you turn on audit logs, your organization's [Enterprise license](../license/page.mdx) includes the `audit` feature, and Medusa sets up the [Audit Module](!resources!/enterprise/audit) in your environments with Cloud's audit storage.

Developers can customize what Medusa records in your environments, such as recording more routes or recording actions from scheduled jobs. Learn more in the [Record API Requests](!resources!/enterprise/audit/record-api-requests) and [Record Custom Audit Logs](!resources!/enterprise/audit/record-custom-audit-logs) guides.

## Turn Off Audit Logs

<RolesBadge roles={["billing"]} />

To turn off audit logs for your organization:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../page.mdx#switch-organization).
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Audit log** in the sidebar.
4. Click the **Turn off audit logs** button at the top right of the page.
5. Click **Turn off** in the confirmation dialog.

When you turn off audit logs:

- Medusa stops recording Cloud activity and hides the audit log.
- The monthly fee stops from your next billing cycle.
- Environments stop recording commerce activity after their next deployment. To stop recording right away, [redeploy your environments](../../deployments/page.mdx).

If you turn audit logs on again later, you only see audit logs recorded after you turned them on again.
