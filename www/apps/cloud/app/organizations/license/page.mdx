# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn how to view your organization's license, the features it includes, and how to use its license key in local development.

## What is a License?

A license unlocks licensed Medusa features for your organization. Your organization's License settings page shows whether your organization has an active license, its license key, and which licensed features it includes.

The licensed features currently are:

- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- OIDC Authentication

---

## View Your Organization's License

To view your organization's license:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../page.mdx#switch-organization) whose license you want to view.
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Open the organization's settings from the sidebar, then click **License** in the settings navigation.

When your organization has an active license, the page header shows an **Active** badge, and the page has the following sections:

- **License Key**: Shows your organization's license key in an obfuscated form.
- **Included Features**: Lists all licensed features, each with a name, description, and an **Included** or **Not included** badge. The section's header shows how many of the features your license includes.
- **Local Development**: Shows the steps to use your license key when running Medusa locally.

---

## Copy the License Key

To copy your organization's license key:

1. Go to your organization's [License settings page](#view-your-organizations-license).
2. In the **License Key** section, click the **Copy** button.

The license key is now copied to your clipboard.

---

## Use the License Key in Local Development

To use your licensed features while developing your Medusa application locally, set the license key in the `MEDUSA_LICENSE_KEY` environment variable of your local Medusa application.

For example, add the following to your Medusa application's `.env` file:

```bash title=".env"
MEDUSA_LICENSE_KEY=<your-license-key>
```

Where `<your-license-key>` is the license key you [copied from the License settings page](#copy-the-license-key).

The **Local Development** section of the License settings page shows these instructions as well.

---

## Get a License

If your organization doesn't have a license, the License settings page shows a card prompting you to contact the Medusa team. The **Included Features** section still lists the licensed features, without inclusion badges, so you can see which features a license unlocks.

To get a license, click the **Contact sales** button on the License settings page. This opens the [Medusa contact page](https://medusajs.com/contact/) in a new tab.
