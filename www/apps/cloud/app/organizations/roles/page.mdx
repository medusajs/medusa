# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn about role-based access control (RBAC) in Cloud, the roles Cloud provides, and how to manage the roles of your organization's members and access keys.

## What is Role-Based Access Control in Cloud?

Role-based access control (RBAC) lets you decide what each member of your organization can view and do in Cloud. Instead of giving every member full access, you assign each member a role, and the role determines the pages they can open and the actions they can perform.

Each member of an organization has exactly one role. Organization [access keys](../../access-keys/page.mdx#manage-organization-access-keys) also have a role, which determines what requests authenticated with the key can do.

When a member opens a page that their role doesn't allow, Cloud shows a **You don't have access to this page** message instead of the page's content. Actions that the member's role doesn't allow are hidden or disabled.

<Note>

Roles in Cloud control access to the Cloud dashboard, the [Cloud CLI](../../cli/page.mdx), and Cloud APIs. They're different from [RBAC in your Medusa application](!resources!/enterprise/rbac) that's available on the **Scale** and **Enterprise** plans, which controls what admin users can do in the Medusa Admin.

</Note>

When you create an organization, you become its owner with the **Admin** role. Members and organization access keys that existed before Cloud introduced roles also have the **Admin** role.

### Permissions Shared by All Roles

Regardless of their role, every member of an organization can:

- View the organization's details.
- View the organization's plan and the features it includes.
- View and update their [notifications](../../notifications/page.mdx).
- [Leave the organization](../page.mdx#leave-organization).

### Admin Role

The Admin role has full access to the organization. Members with this role can:

- View, create, update, and delete all projects, environments, and storefronts, and all of their resources.
- Trigger builds and deployments, and view logs.
- Manage environment variables, vault secrets, custom domains, webhooks, emails, payment accounts, and integration connections.
- Create and manage database exports.
- Manage the organization's plan, add-ons, payment methods, billing address, and invoices.
- View the organization's usage, trial, and credits.
- Invite, update, and remove members, and assign any role to them.
- Create and manage organization access keys with any role.
- View the organization's audit logs.
- Update the organization's details.

### Developer Role

The Developer role is for members who build and deploy the organization's projects. Members with this role can:

- View, create, update, and delete projects and environments.
- Trigger builds and deployments, and view logs.
- Create, update, and delete storefronts. They can create and update storefront environments, and view storefront deployments, but they can't delete storefront environments.
- Manage environment variables, vault secrets, custom domains, webhooks, and integration connections.
- View emails, and update the email account of an environment.
- View, create, and update payment accounts.
- Create and manage database exports.
- Use the [Cloud Assistant](../../assistant/page.mdx).
- View the organization's usage and credits.
- Invite, update, and remove members with the **Developer** role.
- Create and manage organization access keys with the **Developer** role.
- Update the organization's details.

Members with the Developer role can't change the organization's plan, buy add-ons, manage payment methods, billing addresses, or invoices, or view audit logs.

### Billing Role

The Billing role is for members who manage the organization's subscription and payments. Members with this role can:

- Change the organization's plan.
- Manage payment methods and the billing address.
- View invoices and retry failed payments.
- View the organization's usage, trial, and credits. They can also view the list of projects to filter the usage by project.
- Update the organization's details, such as its legal name.

Members with the Billing role can't view or manage project environments, deployments, or their resources. They also can't view the organization's members, manage access keys, or view audit logs.

## Which Roles Can You Assign?

You can only assign a role whose permissions you already have. This prevents members from giving others, or themselves, more access than they have.

In practice, this means:

- Members with the **Admin** role can assign any role.
- Members with the **Developer** role can only assign the **Developer** role.

This rule applies when you invite members, update a member's role, and create organization access keys. The role dropdown only shows the roles you can assign.

If a member or a pending invitation has a role you can't assign, you can't update their role, remove them, resend their invitation, or revoke it.

## Assign a Role When Inviting a Member

<RolesBadge roles={["developer"]} />

You choose a member's role when you invite them to the organization. The member gets the role once they accept the invitation.

To invite a member with a role:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../page.mdx#switch-organization) you want to invite members to.
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Team** in the sidebar.
4. In the **Invite members** section, enter the email address of the user you want to invite.
5. Choose the member's role from the **Role** dropdown.
6. Click **Invite**.

Learn more about managing invitations in the [Organizations](../page.mdx#invite-members-to-organization) guide.

## Update a Member's Role

<RolesBadge roles={["developer"]} />

You can change the role of an existing member at any time. The member's new role replaces their previous one.

<Note>

You can't change the role of the organization's owner. The owner always has the **Admin** role.

</Note>

To update a member's role:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../page.mdx#switch-organization) whose members you want to manage.
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Team** in the sidebar.
4. In the **Organization members** section, find the member whose role you want to update.
5. Click the <InlineIcon Icon={EllipsisHorizontal} alt="three dots" /> icon at the end of the member's row.
6. Choose **Update role** from the dropdown.
7. In the modal that opens, choose the new role from the **Role** dropdown.
8. Click **Save**.

---

## Organization Owner

The organization's owner is the member who created the organization, or the member that the previous owner promoted. The owner has the **Admin** role, and their role can't be changed.

Only the owner can [promote another member to owner](../page.mdx#promote-a-member-to-owner). When the owner promotes a member, that member gets the **Admin** role and becomes the new owner, and the previous owner loses their owner permissions.

## Assign a Role to an Organization Access Key

<RolesBadge roles={["developer"]} />

Every organization access key has a role, which you choose when you create the key. Requests authenticated with the key can only perform the actions that the role allows.

Learn how to create an organization access key with a role in the [Access Keys](../../access-keys/page.mdx#create-organization-access-key) guide.
