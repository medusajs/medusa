# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn how to set up payments for your organization, either by connecting your Stripe account or by using Medusa Managed Payments.

## Open Payments Settings

To open your organization's payments settings:

1. If you're in a different organization, [switch to the organization](../page.mdx#switch-organization) whose payments you want to manage.
2. Make sure you're on the projects overview page of the organization. You can do this by clicking on the organization name at the top left of the dashboard.
3. Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then click **Payments** under the **Services** section.

If your organization has no connected payment provider, the page shows the payments setup options. Otherwise, it shows your connected accounts.

---

## Payment Options

The payments setup page shows two options side by side:

- **Connect with Stripe**: Connect your own Stripe account to your organization. This option is only shown if the Stripe integration is available for your organization.
- **Medusa Managed Payments**: A payment account that Medusa manages for you. The card describes this option, and doesn't have an action button.

---

## Connect a Stripe Account

To connect a Stripe account:

1. [Open the payments settings](#open-payments-settings).
2. In the **Connect with Stripe** card, click **Connect Stripe**.
3. Cloud redirects you to Stripe. Follow the steps on Stripe to authorize the connection.

After you finish or cancel the authorization, Stripe redirects you back to Cloud. A notification shows whether the connection succeeded, was canceled, or failed.

---

## View Connected Stripe Accounts

Once you connect one or more Stripe accounts, the payments settings page shows a card for each account. Each card shows:

- The account's name or display name.
- A status badge, which is one of **Active**, **Onboarding**, **Inactive**, or **Setting Up**.
- The Stripe account ID.

To manage your accounts in Stripe, click the **Open Stripe Dashboard** button. Stripe's dashboard opens in a new tab.

### Configure the Storefront

For an active account, the card shows a code snippet with the environment variables that your storefront needs to accept payments with the account:

- `NEXT_PUBLIC_MEDUSA_PAYMENTS_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`
- `MEDUSA_PAYMENTS_ACCOUNT_ID`

Copy these variables and set them in your storefront's environment. Learn how in the [Environment Variables](../../environments/environment-variables/page.mdx) guide.

If the account isn't active, the card shows a hint about the account's status instead of the snippet.

### Disconnect a Stripe Account

To disconnect a Stripe account:

1. On the account's card, click **Disconnect**.
2. Confirm disconnecting the account in the dialog.

---

## Medusa Managed Payments

If your organization uses Medusa Managed Payments, the payments settings include pages to manage the account, its payouts, and its onboarding.

### Account Settings

The Account Settings page shows a summary card of the managed account that includes:

- The account's email and ID.
- A status badge.
- Whether **Charges** and **Payouts** are enabled for the account.

If the account is still in onboarding, the summary card shows a hint message and an **Apply Now** button that opens the [onboarding page](#complete-onboarding).

Once the account is active, the page also shows:

- A **Storefront integration** section with the environment variables that your storefront needs, as explained in the [Configure the Storefront](#configure-the-storefront) section.
- An **Account Details** section where you manage the account's details and view notifications about actions the account requires.

### Complete Onboarding

The Payment Onboarding page guides you through the steps that Stripe requires to activate your account. Once you exit the onboarding, Cloud takes you to the [Account Settings](#account-settings) page.

### View Payouts and Payments

The Money page shows your managed account's payouts and payments.

<Note>

If the account isn't active yet, the page shows a message and a **Go to Account** button instead. Click the button to go to the [Account Settings](#account-settings) page and complete the account's setup.

</Note>

Once the account is active, the page has two sections:

- **Payouts**: View and manage the payouts to your bank account.
- **Payments**: View the payments that your account received.
