In this guide, you'll learn how to add the Claude Connector to Claude and use it to ask questions about your Medusa stores.

## What is the Medusa Claude Connector?

The Claude Connector is Medusa's listing in [Claude's connectors directory](https://claude.ai/directory/medusa). It connects Claude to the [Medusa MCP server](../page.mdx), so you can ask Claude about your stores' orders, products, customers, and other commerce data. In stores with [read and write access](../page.mdx#read-and-write-access), Claude can also create, update, and delete data, such as products, orders, and promotions.

<Note>

[Rebuild](../page.mdx#step-1-rebuild-environments) any environment whose latest build is from before October 8, 2026 before you connect it to Claude.

</Note>

## Add the Claude Connector

To add the Claude Connector:

1. Open the [Medusa listing](https://claude.ai/directory/medusa) in Claude's connectors directory, or go to **Customize** > **Connectors** in Claude and search for `Medusa`.
2. Click **Connect**.
3. In the browser window that opens, log in to your Medusa account.
4. On the [consent page](../page.mdx#approve-the-connection), select the environments that Claude can access and whether it can change data in each, then click **Approve**.

<Note>

On Claude Team and Enterprise plans, a Claude organization owner may need to add the Claude Connector before members can connect.

</Note>

## Use the Medusa Connector

Enable the connector in a chat from the tools menu in the message input, then ask Claude about your stores. For example:

- "Which Medusa stores do I have connected?"
- "Show me the 5 most recent orders in my store, with each order's total and status."
- "What products are available to shoppers in my B2B sales channel?"
- "Create a 10% off promotion with the code `WINTER10` in my staging store."
- "How do I add a custom field to products in Medusa?"

Claude shows each tool it calls while answering. Refer to the [Medusa MCP](../page.mdx#available-tools) guide for the list of tools.

---

## Remove the Medusa Connector

To remove the Claude Connector:

1. In Claude, go to **Customize** > **Connectors**.
2. Find the Medusa connector and click **Disconnect**.

To change which stores Claude can access or their access levels, [change the connector's environments](../../../organizations/connections/page.mdx#change-environments-and-access) from your organization's **MCP and Connectors** settings, or disconnect and reconnect the connector, then select the environments on the consent page.
