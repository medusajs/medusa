# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn about the AI tools that Medusa provides to build, deploy, and manage your store with AI agents and Large Language Models (LLMs).

## What Makes Medusa AI-Native?

A common issue with traditional commerce platforms is that they aren't built to support AI agents. This leads to unreliable implementations, security risks, and agents that don't understand the platform's architecture.

Medusa's architecture, Framework, and AI tools support developers and non-technical store owners in using AI agents to build stores efficiently and securely, and go live faster. Anything you can do with Medusa, your AI agent can do too.

---

## Get Started with an AI Agent

To create a store with your AI agent, run the following prompt in your agent:

```bash title="Prompt"
Fetch https://docs.medusajs.com/start and create an ecommerce store with Medusa Cloud
```

The AI agent creates your Medusa store and helps you build your custom commerce features.

---

## What You Can Do with the AI Toolkit

With the tools in the AI Toolkit, you and your AI agents can:

1. Create and deploy a Medusa store.
2. Debug its builds and deployments.
3. Build features tailored to your business, without worrying about the complexities of the platform's architecture.
4. Build storefronts that follow commerce best practices and provide a great customer experience.
5. Ask about your store's orders, products, customers, and custom data in natural language.
6. Find accurate answers from the Medusa documentation while you build.
