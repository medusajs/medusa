# {metadata.title}

In this document, you'll learn about gift cards and how they work in the Loyalty Plugin.

## Overview

Gift cards in the Loyalty Plugin provide a flexible way for customers to purchase and redeem credits in your store. Gift cards can be physical products that customers purchase, or they can be issued by admins for promotions, refunds, or customer service purposes.

## Key Concepts

### Gift Card Entity

A gift card represents a stored value that can be redeemed during checkout. Each gift card has:

- **Unique Code**: A unique identifier used to redeem the gift card
- **Amount**: The monetary value of the gift card in the smallest currency unit (e.g., cents)
- **Balance**: The remaining value that can be used (decreases with each redemption)
- **Currency**: The currency code for the gift card value
- **Status**: Active, inactive, or expired
- **Metadata**: Custom fields for additional information

### Gift Card Products

Gift cards can be sold as products in your store. When a customer purchases a gift card product:

1. A gift card entity is automatically created during order fulfillment
2. The gift card code is generated and can be sent to the recipient
3. The gift card becomes available for redemption

### Gift Card Lifecycle

#### 1. Creation
Gift cards can be created in several ways:
- **Product Purchase**: Automatically created when a gift card product is ordered
- **Admin Creation**: Manually created by admins for promotions or customer service
- **API Creation**: Programmatically created through workflows

#### 2. Activation
Once created, gift cards are typically active by default and ready for redemption.

#### 3. Redemption
During checkout, customers can:
- Enter gift card codes to apply them to their cart
- Use multiple gift cards on a single order
- Use gift cards in combination with other payment methods

#### 4. Balance Tracking
- Each redemption reduces the gift card balance
- Gift cards with zero balance cannot be used further
- Partial redemptions are supported

### Gift Card Integration with Orders

When gift cards are redeemed during checkout:

1. **Cart Application**: Gift cards are applied to the cart, reducing the total amount due
2. **Payment Processing**: Gift card redemptions are processed as payment transactions
3. **Order Completion**: Gift card usage is recorded in the order history
4. **Balance Updates**: Gift card balances are automatically updated

### Gift Card Administration

Admins can manage gift cards through:

- **Dashboard Interface**: View, create, and manage gift cards through the admin UI
- **API Endpoints**: Programmatic management through REST APIs
- **Bulk Operations**: Create multiple gift cards at once for campaigns
- **Transaction History**: View all redemption history for each gift card

## Use Cases

### Customer Gifts
Customers can purchase gift cards to give to friends and family, expanding your customer base through referrals.

### Promotions and Marketing
- Issue gift cards as promotional rewards
- Create gift card campaigns for special events
- Offer gift cards as compensation for service issues

### Refunds and Returns
Use gift cards as an alternative to cash refunds, encouraging customers to make future purchases.

### Corporate Sales
Sell gift cards in bulk to businesses for employee rewards or promotional giveaways.

## Best Practices

### Security
- Gift card codes should be sufficiently random to prevent guessing
- Consider implementing usage limits or expiration dates
- Monitor for suspicious redemption patterns

### Customer Experience
- Provide clear instructions on how to redeem gift cards
- Send gift card codes through secure channels (email, SMS)
- Allow customers to check their gift card balance

### Business Operations
- Track gift card liability for accounting purposes
- Set clear terms and conditions for gift card usage
- Consider implementing expiration policies based on local regulations
