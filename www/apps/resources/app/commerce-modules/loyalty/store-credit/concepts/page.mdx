# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn about store credit accounts and how they work in the Loyalty Plugin.

## Overview

Store credit provides a way to give customers account-based credit that can be used for future purchases. Unlike gift cards, store credit is tied to specific customer accounts and provides a running balance that can be credited and debited over time.

For example, you can use store credit to:

- Issue refunds that customers can use for future purchases
- Reward loyal customers with account credits
- Allow customers to accumulate credit from multiple transactions

---

## Store Credit Account

A store credit account represents a customer's credit balance. Each account has:

- **Customer Association**: Linked to a specific customer account
- **Currency**: The currency for the account balance
- **Balance**: Current available credit amount (calculated from transaction history)
- **Status**: Active, inactive, or suspended
- **Metadata**: Custom fields for additional tracking

### Account Transactions

All store credit activity is tracked through transactions that record:

- **Type**: Credit (adding funds) or Debit (spending funds)
- **Amount**: The transaction amount in the smallest currency unit
- **Reference**: What triggered the transaction (order, refund, promotion, etc.)
- **Reference ID**: The ID of the related entity (order ID, return ID, etc.)
- **Note**: Optional description of the transaction
- **Timestamp**: When the transaction occurred

### Balance Calculation

Account balances are calculated in real-time by:
1. Summing all credit transactions
2. Subtracting all debit transactions
3. The result is the current available balance

This approach provides:
- **Accurate Balances**: Always up-to-date with the latest transactions
- **Transaction History**: Complete audit trail of all account activity
- **Reliability**: Prevents balance inconsistencies

## Store Credit Lifecycle

### 1. Account Creation
Store credit accounts can be created:
- **Automatically**: When a customer first receives store credit
- **Manually**: By admins through the dashboard
- **Programmatically**: Through API calls or workflows

### 2. Crediting Accounts
Store credit can be added through:
- **Refunds**: When orders are returned or cancelled
- **Promotions**: Marketing campaigns or loyalty rewards
- **Customer Service**: Compensation for service issues
- **Admin Actions**: Manual credits added by store administrators

### 3. Debiting Accounts
Store credit is spent when:
- **Checkout**: Applied as payment during order completion
- **Adjustments**: Manual debits for corrections or fees
- **Expiration**: If store credit expires (implementation dependent)

### 4. Account Management
Ongoing management includes:
- **Balance Monitoring**: Tracking available credit
- **Transaction History**: Reviewing all account activity
- **Status Changes**: Activating, deactivating, or suspending accounts

## Integration with Orders

Store credit integrates with the order process:

### During Checkout
1. **Application**: Customers can apply available store credit to their cart
2. **Validation**: System verifies sufficient balance is available
3. **Reservation**: Credit is temporarily reserved during payment processing
4. **Confirmation**: Credit is debited when the order is confirmed

### After Order Changes
- **Refunds**: Cancelled or returned orders can credit the account
- **Adjustments**: Order modifications may trigger credit/debit transactions
- **Partial Usage**: Unused store credit remains available for future orders

## Use Cases

### Customer Retention
- Provide store credit instead of cash refunds
- Reward loyal customers with account credits
- Offer credits for referrals or reviews

### Promotional Campaigns
- Issue store credit for marketing campaigns
- Provide credits for first-time customers
- Create loyalty program rewards

### Customer Service
- Compensate customers for service issues
- Provide credits for shipping delays or damaged products
- Resolve disputes with account credits

### Business Operations
- Manage returns and refunds efficiently
- Reduce cash refund processing costs
- Increase customer lifetime value through retained funds

## Best Practices

### Security and Fraud Prevention
- **Access Controls**: Limit who can credit/debit accounts
- **Transaction Limits**: Set maximum transaction amounts
- **Audit Trails**: Maintain complete transaction histories
- **Monitoring**: Watch for unusual account activity

### Customer Experience
- **Balance Visibility**: Show customers their current balance
- **Transaction History**: Let customers review their account activity
- **Clear Communication**: Explain how store credit works
- **Easy Application**: Make it simple to use credit during checkout

### Business Management
- **Regular Reconciliation**: Verify transaction accuracy
- **Liability Tracking**: Monitor total outstanding credit
- **Expiration Policies**: Consider credit expiration rules
- **Integration Testing**: Ensure proper order integration
