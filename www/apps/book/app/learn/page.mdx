## What is Medusa Commerce?

Medusa Commerce is an open-source AI-native commerce platform with a Framework to build custom commerce features.

Medusa Commerce ships with three main tools:

1. A suite of [Commerce Modules](!resources!/commerce-modules) with core commerce functionalities, such as tracking inventory, calculating cart totals, accepting payments, managing orders, and much more.
2. A [Framework](./fundamentals/framework/page.mdx) for building agentic flows and custom functionalities specific to your business, product, or industry. This includes tools for introducing custom API endpoints, business logic, and data models; building workflows and automations; and integrating with third-party services.
3. A customizable [admin dashboard](./fundamentals/admin/page.mdx) for merchants to configure and operate their store.

When you set up Medusa, you get a fully fledged commerce platform with all the features you need to get off the ground. And unlike other platforms, Medusa doesn't require hacky workarounds that are difficult to maintain and scale.

Since Medusa is open source, you can read the source code of any Commerce Module or Framework tool, [contribute fixes and features](./resources/contribution-guidelines/code/page.mdx), and run your Medusa application on any infrastructure. The recommended way to run it is on [Cloud](!cloud!), which deploys your Medusa application and manages its infrastructure.

## Who is This Documentation For?

The chapters in this documentation are for developers who build, customize, and maintain Medusa applications. They assume you're familiar with JavaScript or TypeScript and Node.js.

<Note title="Build with AI agents">

AI agents can build your customizations with the Framework. Learn how to give your agent Medusa's skills and docs in the [Setting Up AI Agents](!cloud!/ai-toolkit) guide.

</Note>

Depending on what you're working on, start with the chapters that fit your needs:

- **New to Medusa:** Follow the Getting Started and [Customization Tutorial](./customization/custom-features/page.mdx) chapters to learn Medusa's architecture and build your first customizations.
- **Building custom features:** The [Basics](./fundamentals/framework/page.mdx) chapter explains the Framework's concepts, such as modules, workflows, and API routes, in depth.
- **Customizing the Medusa Admin:** The [Admin Development](./fundamentals/admin/page.mdx) chapter shows you how to add widgets and UI routes to the admin dashboard.
- **Configuring and maintaining your application:** The [Configurations](./configurations/medusa-config/page.mdx), [Debugging & Testing](./debugging-and-testing/testing-tools/page.mdx), and [Best Practices](./best-practices/third-party-sync/page.mdx) chapters help you configure, test, and debug your Medusa application.
- **Production and upgrading:** The [Production](./build/page.mdx) and [Upgrade](./update/page.mdx) chapters cover building your application for production and updating Medusa.
