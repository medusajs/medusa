# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn how to manage policies in your store using Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

<Note>

This feature is available under the `rbac` feature flag. Learn how to enable it in the [Feature Flags guide](!docs!/learn/debugging-and-testing/feature-flags).

</Note>

## What are Policies?

Policies define specific permissions that control what actions can be performed in your store. Each policy grants permission for a particular operation (like "create", "read", "update", "delete") on a specific resource (like "product", "order", "customer").

---

## View Policies

You can view all admin policies by going to Settings → Users → Policies. In the list, you can see policy details such as the name, resource, operation, and description. You can also search, filter, and sort policies to find specific items.

---

## View Policy Details

To view the details of a policy:

1. Go to Settings → Users → Policies.
2. Click on the policy in the list.

This opens the policy's details page where you can also manage the policy.

---

## Create a New Policy

To create a new policy:

1. Go to Settings → Users → Policies.
2. Click the Create button.
3. In the form that opens:
    - Enter the policy's unique key (used for identification).
    - Enter the policy's display name.
    - Select the resource this policy applies to (e.g., "product", "order").
    - Select the operation this policy allows (e.g., "create", "read", "update", "delete").
    - Optionally, enter a description.
4. Click the Create button.

The policy is created and you're taken to its details page.

---

## Edit Policy Details

To edit a policy's details:

1. Go to the policy's details page.
2. In the General section, click the three-dots icon and choose Edit.
3. In the form that opens, edit the policy's name, resource, operation, and description.
4. Click Save.

<Note>

You cannot change a policy's unique key after creation. The key is used to identify the policy throughout the system.

</Note>

---

## View Policy Usage

To see which roles include a specific policy:

1. Go to the policy's details page.
2. In the Roles section, you'll see all roles that include this policy.

This helps you understand the impact of modifying or deleting a policy.

---

## Delete a Policy

<Note type="warning">

Deleting a policy is irreversible. All roles that include this policy will lose that permission.

</Note>

To delete a policy:

1. Go to Settings → Users → Policies.
2. Find the policy in the list and click on it.
3. In the General section, click the three-dots icon and choose Delete.
4. Confirm deletion by entering the policy's key and clicking Delete.
