# {metadata.title}

<EnterpriseNotice />

In this guide, you'll learn how to manage roles in the Medusa Admin.

<Note title="Enterprise feature">

Roles are part of the role-based access control (RBAC) feature, which is an enterprise feature. A developer must enable it in your Medusa configuration before the **Roles** and **Policies** settings appear. If you don't find these settings, reach out to your development team.

</Note>

## What is a Role?

A role groups a set of permissions, called policies, under a name such as "Product Manager". You assign roles to admin users to control what they can access and do in the Medusa Admin.

---

## When You Don't Have Access

Your roles decide which parts of the Medusa Admin you can open. When you try to open a page that your roles don't cover, the dashboard shows an **Access Denied** message instead of the page, along with the permission the page requires. Nothing is broken, and you can keep using the pages you do have access to.

If your account has no permissions at all, the dashboard sends you to a **No permissions yet** page after you log in, instead of a section of the store. Reach out to your administrator to request access to the areas you need.

When you have at least one permission, the dashboard opens the first section you can access after you log in, such as **Orders** or **Products**.

---

## View Roles

<PermissionsBadge permissions={["rbac_role:read"]} />

To view the roles in your store, go to Settings → Roles. In the list, you can see each role's name and description. You can also search and sort the roles to find a specific one.

To view a role's details, click on it in the list. The role's details page shows its general information, the users assigned to it, and the permissions it grants. To see the assigned policies, you also need the `rbac_policy:read` policy. To see the assigned users, you also need the `user:read` and `rbac_role_assignment:read` policies.

---

## Create a Role

<PermissionsBadge permissions={["rbac_role:create", "rbac_policy:read"]} />

To create a role:

1. Go to Settings → Roles.
2. Click the **Create** button in the top-right corner.
3. In the **Details** tab, enter a name and an optional description for the role.
4. Click **Continue** to move to the **Permissions** tab.
5. Select the permissions that this role should grant. Each permission allows an operation, such as read or create, on a resource, such as products or orders.
    - To learn which policies common roles need, refer to the [Common Roles](./common-roles/page.mdx) guide.
6. Click **Create**.

The role now appears in the list, and you can assign it to users.

<Note>

The **Permissions** tab only lists the policies that you hold yourself. So, you can't create a role that grants more access than you have.

</Note>

---

## Edit a Role's Details

<PermissionsBadge permissions={["rbac_role:update"]} />

To edit a role's name or description:

1. Go to the role's details page.
2. Click the <InlineIcon Icon={EllipsisHorizontal} alt="three-dots" /> icon at the top-right of the first section.
3. Choose **Edit** from the dropdown.
4. In the side window that opens, edit the role's name or description.
5. Click **Save**.

---

## Manage a Role's Permissions

<PermissionsBadge permissions={["rbac_role:update", "rbac_policy:read"]} />

To change the permissions that a role grants:

1. Go to the role's details page.
2. Click the <InlineIcon Icon={EllipsisHorizontal} alt="three-dots" /> icon at the top-right of the first section.
3. Choose **Manage permissions** from the dropdown.
4. In the form that opens, select or unselect the permissions for this role. You can use the search to find specific permissions.
5. Click **Save**.

Users assigned this role gain or lose access based on the permissions you change.

<Note>

The form only lists the policies that you hold yourself, so you can't add or remove policies that you don't have. When you save, the role keeps any policies it has that you don't hold.

</Note>

---

## Assign Users to a Role

<PermissionsBadge permissions={["user:update", "rbac_role_assignment:create", "user:read", "rbac_role_assignment:read"]} />

To assign users to a role:

1. Go to the role's details page.
2. In the **Users** section, click the **Add** button.
3. Select the users you want to assign this role to. You can't select users who already have the role.
4. Click **Save**.

<Note>

You can only assign a role if you have all the permissions that the role grants. If you don't, the **Add** button doesn't appear in the **Users** section.

</Note>

---

## Remove Users from a Role

<PermissionsBadge permissions={["user:update", "rbac_role_assignment:delete", "user:read", "rbac_role_assignment:read"]} />

To remove a user from a role, find the user in the **Users** section, click the <InlineIcon Icon={EllipsisHorizontal} alt="three-dots" /> icon at the end of their row, and choose **Remove**. Then, click **Continue** in the confirmation pop-up.

To remove multiple users at once, select the users in the **Users** section, then click the **Remove** button in the command bar at the bottom of the page. Click **Continue** to confirm.

<Note>

As with assigning users, you can only remove users from a role if you have all the permissions that the role grants.

You also can't remove the last user that has the **Super Admin** role. Assign the **Super Admin** role to another user first.

</Note>

---

## View a User's Roles

<PermissionsBadge permissions={["user:read", "rbac_role:read", "rbac_role_assignment:read"]} />

You can also see the roles of a specific user:

1. Go to Settings → Users.
2. Click on the user in the list.
3. Scroll to the **Roles** section, which lists every role assigned to that user.

Click on a role in the list to go to its details page, where you can change its permissions or the users assigned to it.

---

## Delete a Role

<PermissionsBadge permissions={["rbac_role:delete"]} />

<Note type="warning">

Deleting a role is irreversible. Users assigned to the role lose the access it granted.

</Note>

<Note>

You can't delete the built-in **Super Admin** role.

</Note>

To delete a role:

1. Go to the role's details page.
2. Click the <InlineIcon Icon={EllipsisHorizontal} alt="three-dots" /> icon at the top-right of the first section.
3. Choose **Delete** from the dropdown.
4. Confirm deleting the role in the pop-up.

You can also delete a role from the Settings → Roles list. Click the <InlineIcon Icon={EllipsisHorizontal} alt="three-dots" /> icon at the end of the role's row and choose **Delete**.
