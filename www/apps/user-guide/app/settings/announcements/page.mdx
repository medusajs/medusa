# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn how to choose the announcements that your team sees in the Medusa Admin.

<Note>

Announcements are available from [Medusa v2.22.7](https://github.com/medusajs/medusa/releases/tag/v2.22.7). If you don't see the Announcements settings in your Medusa Admin, request your technical team to [upgrade your Medusa application](!docs!/learn/update).

</Note>

## Overview

Medusa shares announcements in the Medusa Admin, such as security updates, new features, and tips for the page you're on. You find them in two places:

- **What's new**: Click the megaphone icon at the top of the dashboard to open a side window that lists the latest announcements. A dot on the icon and a **New** badge indicate announcements you haven't seen yet.
- **Sidebar card**: A card above the **Settings** item in the sidebar shows a new announcement until you dismiss it.

To read more about an announcement, click **View details** or **Learn more**.

In the Announcements settings, you choose which categories of announcements appear in both places.

## Enable or Disable an Announcement Category

<PermissionsBadge permissions={["cloud_announcement:update"]} />

To enable or disable an announcement category:

1. Go to Settings → Announcements.
2. Click the toggle next to the category.

The dashboard saves the change right away and shows a confirmation message. If you can't click the toggles, you don't have the permission to change the announcement settings.

<Note title="Warning" type="warning">

Announcement settings apply to your entire team, not only to your account. When you disable a category, no one on your team sees its announcements.

</Note>

After you disable a category, its announcements may still appear for a few minutes.

If you disable all categories, the megaphone icon and the sidebar card no longer appear in the dashboard.

<Note title="Tip">

Keep **Security updates** enabled so that your team learns about security advisories and incidents as soon as Medusa announces them.

</Note>
