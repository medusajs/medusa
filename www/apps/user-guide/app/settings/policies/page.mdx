# {metadata.title}

<EnterpriseNotice />

In this guide, you'll learn how to view policies in the Medusa Admin.

<Note title="Enterprise feature">

Policies are part of the [role-based access control (RBAC) feature](!resources!/commerce-modules/rbac), which is an enterprise feature. A developer must enable it in your Medusa configuration before the **Roles** and **Policies** settings appear.

</Note>

## Overview

A policy is a single permission that allows an operation on a resource. For example, the `order:read` policy allows viewing orders.

Policies are defined by your team and grouped into [roles](../roles/page.mdx) that you assign to users.

---

## View Policies

<PermissionsBadge permissions={["rbac_policy:read"]} />

To view the available policies, go to Settings → Policies. In the list, you can see each policy's key, resource, and operation. You can search and sort the policies to find a specific one.

To see which roles include a policy, click on the policy in the list. The policy's details page shows the roles that grant it. To see those roles, you also need the `rbac_role:read` policy.

<Note>

By default, Medusa defines policies for its built-in resources, such as `user:read`. To add custom policies, ask your developers to [define them in code](!resources!/commerce-modules/rbac/define-policies).

</Note>
